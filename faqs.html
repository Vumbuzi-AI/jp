Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about booking appointments, insurance, telemedicine, and visiting our facilities. Can't find your answer? Reach out to our team.

Have questions?

We've got answers

Browse the most common questions our patients ask. Only one answer opens at a time.

How do I schedule an appointment?

To schedule an appointment with us, you can call our office during business hours, or you can use our online appointment booking system available on our website. Simply select your preferred date and time, and our scheduling team will confirm your appointment.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans. Before your visit, contact our billing team with your provider and policy details, and we'll verify your coverage and explain any out-of-pocket costs so there are no surprises.

Do you offer telemedicine appointments?

Yes. We offer secure virtual visits for non-emergency concerns, follow-ups, and consultations. Book a telemedicine slot through our online system and you'll receive a private video link before your appointment time.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records or referrals. Arriving 15 minutes early helps us complete check-in and paperwork smoothly.

Can I request prescription refills online?

Absolutely. Log in to your patient portal to request refills for eligible medications. Requests are typically reviewed within one business day, and you'll be notified once your prescription is sent to your pharmacy.

What safety measures are in place for in-person appointments?

Your safety is our priority. We maintain rigorous cleaning protocols, provide sanitizing stations throughout the facility, and follow current public health guidance. Staff are trained to keep every visit safe and comfortable.

Still have a question?

Our team is happy to help. Give us a call or send a message and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
