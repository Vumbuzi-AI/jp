J.P. Muia Health System exists to close the gap between the healthcare Kenya deserves and the healthcare that has been available.

Our Story

From Vision to Foundation

Dr. Timothy Muia grew up witnessing the consequences of a fragmented health system — patients referred too late, follow-up lost, specialist care inaccessible. He trained across the world's most advanced health systems with one conviction: Kenya could build this.

J.P. Muia Health System was founded on the belief that Africa's healthcare challenge is not a shortage of compassion, but a shortage of system — one that connects community screening to specialist treatment to follow-up, and uses AI not as a gimmick but as an essential clinical tool.

Konza Technopolis, Kenya's smart city, is the natural home for Africa's first AI-smart hospital.

Our Mission

To build Africa's first AI-smart, research-ready health system — connecting community health, specialist care, and structured follow-up in an integrated, technology-driven ecosystem accessible to all.

Our Vision

A Kenya — and an Africa — where geography, income, and circumstance no longer determine the quality of healthcare a person receives. Where the best of global medicine is available at home.
