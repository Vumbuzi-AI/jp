An Integrated Care Continuum

Most facilities operate as isolated institutions. J.P. Muia treats all levels of care as one connected organism with shared systems, so no patient is lost during referral, discharge, or follow-up.

Level 1: Community Health

Community health workers and JP Muia on Wheels bring screening, NCD detection, prevention, and referral directly to villages.

Level 2: Tertiary Care

Dual catheterization labs, operating theatres, AI diagnostics, and Medtronic technology support specialists in cardiology, oncology, neurobehavioral care, and surgery.

Level 3: Return Pathway

A 48-hour follow-up call, 7-day and 30-day check-ins, and lifelong digital records mean no patient is discharged into silence. "Every person who enters the system should receive care that continues after they return home."
