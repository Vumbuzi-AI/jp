An Integrated System, Not a Standalone Hospital

Most healthcare facilities operate like isolated institutions. J.P. Muia treats every level of care as one connected organism — from village screening to tertiary specialist treatment — so patients are never lost between facilities or after discharge.

The Origin

Dr. Timothy Muia grew up in Ngaamba, a marginalized rural Kenyan village, with a frequently ill mother. Each time she fell sick, the family made a five-hour journey to Nairobi for treatment. That experience — carried through the Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health and into global healthcare networks — shaped his promise to build a world-class health system so families in his region never have to make that journey again.
