The Origin

Where the Vision Was Born

Dr. Timothy Muia grew up witnessing healthcare that fell short of what people deserved. He trained in the world's most advanced systems with one conviction: Kenya could build this. J.P. Muia Health System at Konza Technopolis is the answer — AI meets compassionate care, research meets rural outreach, and world-class treatment becomes accessible to every Kenyan.

Our Vision
“
We are not building a hospital. We are building a health system — one that finds patients before they fall sick, treats them with the best medicine the world has to offer, and follows them home to ensure they truly recover. This is what Kenya deserves.
T
Dr. Timothy Muia
